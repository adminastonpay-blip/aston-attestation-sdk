# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We aim to make participation in ASTON Attestation respectful, constructive and safe for everyone, regardless of experience, identity or background.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good faith while addressing technical disagreements.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence and code, not individuals.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them transparently.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information, credentials or private vulnerability details.
- Follow the project's security reporting process.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, intimidation, sustained disruption, malicious disclosure of private information, or knowingly submitting malicious code or credentials.

## Enforcement
Reports may be sent privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review reports, preserve confidentiality where possible, and may remove content, restrict participation or escalate serious threats. A second maintainer should review enforcement decisions where feasible.

## Current limitation
The project has not yet published a staffed enforcement rota or independent appeals contact. Those must be assigned before claiming mature community-governance operations.
